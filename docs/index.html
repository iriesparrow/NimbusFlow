guide ▾
Part A — Heart Breathing
Breathe into your heart to a count of 4 — visualize breath entering through the center of your chest. Breathe out to a count of 7, exiting through the same point. Repeat 5–10 times (use the Heart visualizer above). Then return to natural breath and repeat:
"I now open my heart fully to all living things. I love all of life."
Part B — The Five Wishes
For each person, visualize them clearly and offer:
1. May you be happy
2. May you be healthy and strong
3. May you be safe, on the inside and out
4. May you awaken to your highest potential
5. May you live with ease

Conclude each person: "I love you, I love you, I love you."

Scientific Prayer — 7 StepsSurrender · Know · Ready · Forgive+Love · Declare · Thank · Devote
guide ▾
Perform immediately after meditation, while vibration is elevated.
1 · I Surrender
Release all preconceived notions. Feel the physiological release of every need, worry, and desire to Universal Intelligence.
2 · I Know
Heighten awareness of your connection to Universal mind. Acknowledge the oneness and the presence.
3 · I Am Ready
Invite the Universe to make whatever changes are required for your evolution. Give full permission for upgrades to limiting beliefs.
4 · I Forgive and I Love
Replace guilt and regret with love. Forgive yourself, forgive others, wish happiness and prosperity to all.
5 · I Declare
State your intentions clearly, in the present tense:
"I trust the abundance already in motion. I receive with ease and calm. The money follows the work I've already done."
6 · I Thank
Step into the future. Experience the reality of each intention already manifested. Celebrate precisely as if it just happened.
7 · I Devote
Reaffirm commitment to your co-creative relationship with the Universe. Move forward with 100% belief, faith, trust, and knowing.
